Pricing questions

How much does a massage cost at J Massage SLC?

Standard massages are $105 for 60 minutes, $155 for 90 minutes, and $205 for 120 minutes. This covers Swedish, sports, Ashiatsu, Shiatsu, and myofascial. A 30-minute massage is $65; call us at (801) 288-1118 to book it. Deep tissue costs the same as any massage right now: no extra charge at any length. Prenatal massage is $105 for 60 minutes, $155 for 90, and $205 for 120. Couples massage and 4-hand massage start at $205 for 60 minutes. All prices are listed in full above, no hidden fees.

Is there a fee for paying by card?

No added fee. Prices shown are card prices. Cash is $5 less.

What add-ons can I include with my massage?

Five add-ons are available: hot stones, cupping and doTERRA essential oils for $20 each, plus infrared sauna (30 minutes) and CBD at $30 each. A 30-minute prenatal extension is $50. Select any of them when booking online or mention them when you call.

Are gift cards available?

Yes, J Massage SLC gift cards are available for any dollar amount or for a specific service. They never expire and make a thoughtful gift for any occasion. Visit our gift cards page or call (801) 288-1118 to purchase.
