FAQ

Pricing questions

How much does a massage cost at J Massage SLC?
Standard massages are $85 for 60 minutes, $125 for 90 minutes, and $165 for 120 minutes. This covers Swedish, deep tissue, sports, Ashiatsu, Shiatsu, and myofascial. Prenatal massage is priced separately at $105 for 60 minutes, $155 for 90, and $205 for 120. Couples massage and 4-hand massage start at $165 for 60 minutes. All prices are listed in full above, no hidden fees.
Is there a fee for paying by card?
A small card processing fee may apply when paying by credit or debit card. There is no additional fee when paying with cash. This is confirmed at the time of booking so there are no surprises.
What add-ons can I include with my massage?
Four add-ons are available: hot stones and cupping for $20 each, plus infrared sauna (30 minutes) and CBD at $30 each. A 30-minute prenatal extension is $50. Select any of them when booking online or mention them when you call.
Are gift cards available?
Yes, J Massage SLC gift cards are available for any dollar amount or for a specific service. They never expire and make a thoughtful gift for any occasion. Visit our gift cards page or call (801) 288-1118 to purchase.
