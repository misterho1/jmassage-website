What Is a Couples Massage?

A couples massage places both partners on side-by-side tables in a single private suite, each attended by their own dedicated licensed therapist. Both sessions begin at precisely the same moment and run in parallel, so you decompress together rather than sequentially, in separate rooms, comparing notes over coffee afterward. At J Massage SLC, your suite is reserved exclusively for you. No shared hallways mid-session. No ambient noise from adjacent rooms. The space is yours, completely, from the moment you arrive.

It's not "two massages happening at the same time." Because both people drop in simultaneously, there's a shared presence you can't manufacture in daily life. Phones away. Obligations gone. Common Questions

Couples Massage FAQ

Do we have to get the same type of massage as our partner?

Absolutely not, and this is one of the things that sets J Massage SLC's couples experience apart. Each person consults individually with their therapist and chooses their preferred modality. One partner can receive a gentle Swedish massage while the other gets a focused deep tissue session. One can request lighter pressure; the other can request firm work on problem areas. Your session is customized to you individually while still being a fully shared experience.

Is the couples suite completely private?

Yes, completely. Your suite is a dedicated, sound-attenuated private room reserved exclusively for you and your partner for the entire duration of your session. Not a partitioned corner of a larger room, an actual dedicated space with its own entrance. No shared waiting rooms, no ambient noise from adjacent rooms, no interruptions. Once you're in, the suite is entirely yours.

Can we request add-ons like hot stones or infrared sauna?

Yes. Hot stone enhancements, deep pressure upgrades, and access to our infrared sauna can all be added to your couples session. These can be added individually, one partner can add hot stones while the other keeps their session as-is, or both can upgrade. Ask about available add-ons when you book, or include a note in your reservation and we'll have everything ready.

Is couples massage a good gift idea?

It's consistently one of the most appreciated gifts in Salt Lake City, full stop. Unlike most luxury gifts, a couples session is genuinely used, genuinely enjoyed, and genuinely remembered. J Massage SLC gift cards for couples sessions are available online, delivered digitally within minutes, and can be redeemed for any session. They're ideal for anniversaries, Valentine's Day, birthdays, weddings, and any occasion where you want to give something meaningful rather than another thing that gets forgotten.
